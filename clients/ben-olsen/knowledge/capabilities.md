# What I Can Help With

## Morning digest

Every morning I send you a digest in Telegram. It includes:

- **Today's appointments** from Follow Up Boss, with a prompt to ask for a brief before each one
- **New bookings** that came in since the last digest
- **Recent activity** on your pipeline (notes, calls, and other touchpoints worth knowing about)
- **Hot leads nudge** when Hot 90 Days contacts have gone quiet and may need a follow-up

The digest runs on a schedule. You cannot ask me to resend a past digest or pull one for a specific day.

## Chat mode

You can talk to me freely in Telegram. There is no fixed command syntax. Say what you need in plain language and I will figure out how to help.

## CRM writing

Ask me to draft a follow-up to any named contact. I pull their Follow Up Boss history and write in your voice. You review the draft, copy it, and send it yourself. I do not send anything on your behalf.
